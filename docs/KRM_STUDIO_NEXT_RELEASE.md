## What changed
**Complete independent redesign, not a reskin.** A distinct KRM studio identity that differs visually from all the example sites.
- Deep near-black canvas, electric violet, soft ultramarine and coral/tangerine signature accents.
- KRM brand monogram, original typographic hero "WE MAKE BRANDS IMPOSSIBLE TO IGNORE", stylized grid, volumetric CSS orb, cursor-reactive light (on supported devices).
- Three full-width **bespoke project narratives** rather than repetitive business cards: NOVA cinematic automotive; VANTA contrasting experimental editorial; ORLA organic parchment Mediterranean.
- Each case links to the latest appropriate, tested concept demonstration.
- A functional **Experience Lab** with three real embedded live websites and desktop/mobile responsive preview controls.
- An interactive, client-side-only **Creative Brief Builder** with three choices (sector, creative direction, objective), generated summary and optional copy-to-clipboard.
- A four-part design process section, custom closing art direction and transparent disclaimers.
- GSAP/ScrollTrigger enhanced motion only when available and reduced-motion preference allows; content remains readable without animation library.
- Mobile navigation, keyboard-focus indicators, scroll progress and lightweight JS components.
- No new image-generation charges or cash spend; reused existing original media and illustrative project photography.

## Production disclaimers / next gates
- NOVA DETAIL, VANTA OFFGRID and ORLA are **fictional design concepts**, not client commissions or real operating businesses.
- Project imagery mixes original digitally generated concept assets and illustrative licensed stock photos, not actual customer work.
- No real restaurant reservations, barber appointments, sales orders, testimonials, performance guarantees or customer data collection.
- The site is currently a **public design showcase**, not an independently registered business with a legal checkout or sales operation. Before commercial use: validate German law (Impressum, Datenschutz, Gewerbe/registration, advertising/communication rules), contact pathway, privacy, image licenses, hosting handover and contracts.
- Browser smoke tests are not a full cross-browser accessibility/WCAG assessment or mobile Lighthouse / Core Web Vitals audit. No invented measured performance scores.
- No new cash spent from the original €50 challenge budget.

## Key difference
This portfolio is intended to communicate the **studio** first: strategy, art direction, creative judgment and tangible interactive output. The three live concept websites become evidence rather than the sole visual structure of the website.
